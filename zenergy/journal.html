June 2026 · Faroe Islands

Waiting for weather that never clears

Four days chasing a break in the cloud that never fully came — and the one frame that made the trip worth it anyway. Flat, grey light is unforgiving: no shadow to build a composition around, no golden edge to fall back on. The fix was leaning into it instead of fighting it — shooting for tone and shape rather than contrast, and letting the color desaturate on its own instead of forcing it in post.

March 2026 · Marrakech

Shooting into the sun and losing half the frames

A week in the medina taught a hard lesson about backlighting: half the roll came back blown out or flared beyond saving. The other half is some of the best work in the whole archive. Worth the trade. Notes on metering for highlights instead of shadows when the light source is directly behind the subject.

January 2026 · Osaka

What a rainy Tuesday does for street photography

Bad weather clears a street of everyone except the people who actually need to be out in it — which turns out to be exactly who's worth photographing. Umbrellas, reflections, and the particular quiet of a city under rain. A short case for shooting on the days you'd normally stay in.
