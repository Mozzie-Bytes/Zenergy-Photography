The project

Shot on instinct, kept on purpose.

Zenergy started as a way to slow down, get off the screens & out of the house — one frame a day, no reshoots, no staging. It's grown into a running record of light: golden hour on a fire escape, fog rolling off a coastline, the half-second before someone notices the camera. Some of it's good. All of it's honest.

This is where it lives, roll by roll.
